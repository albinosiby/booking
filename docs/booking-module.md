# Booking module

## Scope and owner/staff flow

The full-screen New booking page opens from a floating New booking action on Home, Bookings and Calendar. The action stays above the elevated bottom navigation, and page content reserves clearance below the last item. The reference-inspired form presents numbered Customer, Space and Date & time cards. Enter the customer, select horizontal business cards, then sport and space chips. Choose a date from the compact date row or calendar, a configured duration and a start from the availability grid. Customer email/notes and optional split payments are disclosed on demand. Review the quote before confirming. The form remains full-screen. Close/back exits without an unsaved-changes confirmation for both new and rescheduled bookings; unresolved submissions must still be verified before leaving. A fixed bottom panel shows total, net received, balance/refund due and Review/Confirm. Selecting from Calendar pre-fills its business/space/date filters. Time entry accepts/displays midnight as 24:00 and sends canonical 00:00.

Customer name/phone are required; email and notes are optional. Phone lookup suggests accessible existing customers without silently overwriting their saved profile. Booking contact details are snapshots; edits to one booking do not rewrite other bookings' customers. Full payment, partial payment and confirming unpaid are supported. Cash, UPI, card and bank transfer entries each keep an amount, reference, received timestamp and collector. There is no payment gateway or automatic message delivery.

Booking details shows reference, space, local dates/times/timezone, customer, status, agreed total, net received, balance/refund due, payment entries and change history. Authorized staff can add collections or update contact details/notes. Confirmed bookings can be rescheduled to any future available date/time within configured hours, including another assigned space in the same business. The original reservation is replaced only when the new reservation and price snapshot are secured in one transaction. Customer, reference and payment history remain attached. A higher price increases the balance; a lower price than net payments creates refund due.

Cancellation releases inventory, keeps records and makes existing net payments refundable; it does not automatically refund money. Refund recording is owner-only, requires a reason and cannot exceed refund due. Completed/no-show statuses are allowed only after the relevant end/start time, and retain payment obligations. No-show does not release the remaining reserved time early. There is no overpayment collection, price override or tentative reservation in this release.

Paid confirmed regular bookings expose an owner-only **Cancel & refund** action. It confirms cancellation first using the existing status command, refreshes the authoritative booking/version, then opens the existing refund editor. Refunds require a second explicit save. Cash and UPI (including cash collections refunded via UPI) use the refund's selected source, and partial/split refunds remain supported. An uncertain cancellation retries its original command before opening the form. A successful cancellation whose refresh fails clears the stale confirmed record and pending command; reload the booking and use **Record refund** to continue. Staff, unpaid bookings and admission visits do not get the combined action. No API or schema change is required.

## Business-specific inventory

- Turf: football/cricket offerings reserve physical section IDs. Each half is independent; Full Ground reserves both halves and conflicts across sports.
- Courts: one exclusive resource per court, including preparation/cleanup.
- Pools: regular admissions and group offers reserve actual swimmer quantities across the whole interval. Capacity uses the maximum overlapping occupancy at each time boundary, not a sum of nonconcurrent visits. Exact/up-to group rules and prices remain configured per offer. Purchases, plan attendance-day consumption and coaching sessions are not part of this module.
- Halls: hourly/session/full-day offers share one exclusive parent hall. Session/full-day times are fixed by configuration; total package price is separate from hourly tariffs. Guest count must fit capacity.

Rates use booking start time, with dated seasonal precedence where supported. The API requires a fresh price quote/version and rechecks inventory at confirmation. Price changes never alter existing bookings automatically. A stale quote requires review; failed rescheduling preserves the original booking and allocation.

Dates/time entry represent the business location timezone, with timezone shown on the form/details. For overnight opening hours, early-morning bookings use the previous operating weekday for pricing. The calendar includes bookings continuing from the previous day. Ambiguous/nonexistent daylight-saving start times are rejected rather than silently choosing an occurrence. Suggestions use 15-minute starts; manual minute-accurate entry remains available. Payment entry dates/times are labeled device local time and stored as UTC instants; business timezone determines daily collection reporting.

## Screens and permissions

Home shows Today’s bookings, Collected today and Due amount to collect across all accessible businesses, without a business dropdown. The due total covers unpaid balances across all dates. Today’s upcoming bookings are grouped by space beside a separate Payments to collect list on wide screens; the sections stack on mobile and with enlarged text. Empty states have no background card. The collection list excludes refund-only and cancelled bookings, supports loading more balances, and has independent loading/retry states. Collect opens the existing booking payment editor for users with collect_payments; opening it does not record a payment. Successful booking/payment mutations refresh both Home lists and totals. An advance collected today for a future booking appears in today's collections. Bookings supports customer/phone/reference search and business/date/status filters. Calendar shows the selected day’s bookings grouped by space, with search/status/business/space filters available from the app-bar filter sheet. Payments has Collections and Balances & refunds views. Collections shows complete method totals independent of pagination; balances defaults to all booking dates and shows unpaid amounts/refunds due within the user’s assigned scope.

Owners have all actions. Staff need assigned businesses/resources and server-enforced permissions: manage_bookings for mutations/rescheduling, collect_payments for collections, and view_bookings (or booking management/collection responsibility) for booking reads. Finance-only users can see assigned transaction reporting without gaining booking-edit access. Finance totals require view_finance; a booking manager still sees the balance on bookings they manage. Removed memberships and resource scopes cannot access records through IDs, customer lookup or retry recovery.

## Architecture and persistence

Flutter `modules/bookings` contains immutable catalog, selection, quote, customer, payment, record and screen-state models; BookingFormCubit, BookingDetailCubit and BookingListCubit; BookingRepository/RestBookingRepository; full-screen form/details and shared list/calendar/payments views. HomeCubit observes booking changes through the repository. Screens render models and invoke Cubit methods. GetIt owns composition; data transport stays inside repositories/services. Scope changes clear cached screen data; stale asynchronous responses are ignored.

The Nest API stores normalized customers, bookings, inventory allocations, append-only collection/refund entries, booking history and idempotency command records. Migration `002_bookings.sql` is additive. A migration ledger applies ordered SQL files once; existing workspace/configuration records remain intact. Composite foreign keys preserve organization and resource/business ownership. Booking/configuration writes share a business-row lock; collector writes also check the current booking version. Availability displays are advisory; the transaction is authoritative.

Booking creation saves contact snapshot, booking, occupancy and initial split entries atomically. Stable command UUIDs with request hashes make retries replay-safe. The client keeps the exact pending command after an uncertain network response, disables further edits and attempts command-result recovery; Retry sends that same command. Pending drafts/PII are not written to insecure browser storage. The form requires outcome recovery before leaving an uncertain confirmation. Restarting the app clears an unsaved in-memory draft; inspect booking/payment history before manually recreating a potentially committed operation.

Configuration guards prevent deleting spaces with booking history, reducing capacity while future reservations exist or changing occupied turf footprints. Price/hours edits can update future availability while historical booking snapshots remain unchanged. Disable an old space instead of deleting history. All operational collections/searches remain organization/resource scoped; amounts are bounded integer minor units. Lists and ledgers are paginated; aggregate totals include all matching rows. No offline booking writes are supported.

## Verification and deployment

Automated tests cover simultaneous court/collector writes, cash/UPI/card splits, replay protection, partial collections, stale quotes, atomic reschedule failure, price differences/refunds, cross-sport shared turf, pooled capacity, hall option overlap, staff/tenant boundaries, calendar overnight ranges, timezone transitions, aggregate/pagination totals and small-screen enlarged-text form completion. Existing Flutter and API CI checks apply.

Local persistence remains on the Mac in `.local/postgres`, accessed through the Nest API. A pre-migration backup is stored under `.local/backups/pre-bookings-20261015-122950`. Restore only while the API is stopped, and preserve the current database separately before replacing it. Production requires the existing Firebase, PostgreSQL, HTTPS, signing and backup configuration. Run migrations as a single deployment step before serving new application traffic; verify schema/backup recovery in staging. Booking-time writes must continue through this authoritative API when moving storage providers.

## Booking list and calendar presentation

Bookings uses a search field (keyboard Search or arrow to submit), horizontally scrollable status filters and a bottom sheet for business filtering. Cards show space/business, local booking date/time, customer, reference, status, total and payment/balance/refund badges. Calendar uses a compact horizontally scrollable day strip with weekday/day/month labels, per-day booking counts and a highlighted selected day. Its date-jump picker opens from the calendar icon at the far right of the app bar; the strip header keeps the month/year and Today shortcut. The date row scrolls across month and year boundaries; distant selections are brought back into view. Selecting a day immediately refreshes that day’s schedule, grouped by space. Week view has been removed from the UI and Cubit/model. Filters support historical resources and assigned access. The FAB carries the selected business/space/date into the full-screen booking form. Counts describe loaded rows, with a plus when more pages exist, rather than claiming an aggregate total.

Cubit clears prior results when refreshing a filter/date, and its request-generation guard rejects late responses. The selected date therefore does not show another day’s old bookings while loading. Repository and API contracts remain unchanged.

The compact date strip displays a small count below each day. Its repository requests server aggregates for a debounced 31-day window around the visible row. Counts match current business/resource/status/search filters and calendar overlap rules, independent of list pagination. Missing or failed counts show a dash rather than zero; a retry action is provided. Zero appears only for a successfully returned empty date. Count requests have their own generation/scope guard and refresh after booking changes.

Bookings date filtering opens from the calendar icon at the far right of its app bar. The former full-width date button above Search is removed. Choosing a date filters via the existing Cubit/repository; the selected date appears as a removable chip below the search/status controls. Deleting the chip restores all dates; cancelling the picker preserves the current selection. Calendar retains its separate horizontal date strip and counts.

Both Bookings and Calendar now place their date-picker icon at the far right of their app bar. Calendar no longer has an inline date-picker button; its date strip and count badges remain immediately below the page introduction.

Calendar places filter, refresh and date actions in the app bar. Standalone date cards have no enclosing surface/background card. The chosen day’s results follow the strip directly; search/status options live in the filter sheet. Empty days use plain centered text in the remaining viewport, without a background card. Bookings retains its search/status controls and uses the same plain, centered empty-message layout as Calendar, without a background card. Empty lists do not display the loaded-count badge.

A successful empty result shows only the plain empty message; actual repository errors show the existing retryable error and suppress the empty message. Booking-count badges use customer-facing “booking/bookings” labels for populated results.


## Reference-inspired booking form

Business selection uses cards; turf options include explicit sport/format metadata rather than deriving sports from labels. Changing sports preserves customer details and selects a compatible space. Rescheduling keeps the original business and customer, while retaining existing collections.

Availability loads automatically after space/date/duration/participant changes, with a separate generation guard from price requests. Typing customer details or choosing a start cannot cancel or overwrite an in-flight grid. Slot loading/error/retry is inline; late responses for another selection or organization are ignored. Selecting an available start checks the authoritative quote and updates the fixed total. The grid distinguishes Available, Booked/Unavailable and Past. Past starts are display-only: quote/confirmation still reject them. Pool tiles can show remaining places; fixed hall sessions retain their configured times. Exact minute entry remains available alongside the quarter-hour grid.

The form retains allowed duration choices rather than accepting arbitrary finish times. Price, capacity and shared turf-section conflicts are enforced by the existing quote/confirmation APIs. Split payments and command-key retry recovery are unchanged. No database migration is needed for the additive catalog and slot-status response fields.

On desktop, Calendar keeps the page heading, month picker and selected-day heading fixed while the bookings list scrolls independently. Selecting another date starts its bookings at the top. The month pane can scroll independently on short windows so month navigation, count retry, filters and Today remain reachable. Compact screens keep the existing single-column date-strip layout.
